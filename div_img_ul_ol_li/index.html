Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela

South African anti-apartheid revolutionary, political leader, and philanthropist. First black President of South Africa (1994–1999) and a global symbol of resistance, reconciliation, and human rights.

Quick Facts

  • Full name: Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela
  • Born: 18 July 1918
  • Died: 5 December 2013
  • Nationality: South African
  • Known for: Anti-apartheid activism, leadership, presidency, reconciliation efforts

Career & Achievements (selected timeline)

  1. 1944 — Joined the African National Congress (ANC) and co-founded the ANC Youth League.
  2. 1962 — Arrested and later sentenced to life imprisonment for sabotage and other charges.
  3. 1990 — Released from prison after 27 years; became a central figure in negotiations to end apartheid.
  4. 1994 — Elected President of South Africa in the country’s first multiracial elections.
  5. 1995 — Led the country through Truth and Reconciliation efforts and won international respect for his leadership style.
  6. 1999 — Retired from active politics; continued philanthropic and human rights work through the Nelson Mandela Foundation.

Legacy & Quotations

  • Legacy: Symbol of resistance to oppression, global advocate for peace, and a unifying figure in South Africa’s transition from apartheid to democracy.
  • Famous quote: "I have fought against white domination, and I have fought against black domination. I have cherished the ideal of a democratic and free society..."
